About Lumen Wonder

A curated Catholic resource directory and search engine — built to be simple, trustworthy, and careful.

Our goal

Help regular Catholics, families, catechists, and travelers quickly find trusted Catholic resources, places, and practical guidance.

What this site is

  • A curated Catholic resource directory and search engine
  • A place that links people to trusted Catholic websites, organizations, parishes, shrines, podcasts, CCD materials, and pilgrimages
  • A tool that can give short, careful, source-grounded pointers when helpful

What this site is not

  • An official voice of the Catholic Church
  • A replacement for a priest, parish, or diocese
  • A place that teaches doctrine on its own authority

When in doubt, prefer Official Church and Diocesan sources, and speak with your local pastor.

Trust labels

Every resource in the directory carries a trust label. Labels describe who publishes the resource — not a ranking of holiness or usefulness.

How to use Lumen Wonder

  • Search from the homepage for a topic (e.g. Confession, CCD, Lourdes).
  • Find Mass with city/ZIP or device location, then open trusted Mass-finder tools.
  • Browse categories when you know the kind of help you need.
  • Filter the directory by category, trust label, or region.
  • Check the trust label before relying on a source for teaching or pastoral decisions.
  • Visit the original site — we point you there; we do not replace it.
  • Suggest a resource if you know something solid we should review.

Growing the directory

Resources live in a simple data file. Anyone can suggest an addition; editors review and paste approved entries into the directory. Future versions may add careful short answers with sources and a full review CMS.
